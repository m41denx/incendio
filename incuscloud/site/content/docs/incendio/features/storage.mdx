Under **Storage** you'll find **Pools**, **Volumes**, **Buckets** and **Custom ISOs**.

<Screenshot name="storage-pools" />

## Pools

Create a pool on any driver Incus has: Dir, Btrfs, LVM (including clustered LVM), ZFS, Ceph,
CephFS, Ceph Object, **LINSTOR** and **TrueNAS**, each with its own settings. Drivers your server
can't use are greyed out.

For ZFS, a **vdev builder** composes the pool's source: pick stripe, mirror, raidz1 or raidz2 and
list the block devices.

## Volumes

- **Btrfs compression** (zstd, lzo, zlib or none) per volume.
- **Initial owner**: the uid, gid and mode a new filesystem volume starts with.
- A **Files** tab on custom filesystem volumes to browse, download and upload files (several at
  once, with progress and an overwrite warning), create folders and delete, without attaching the
  volume to an instance.

<Screenshot name="btrfs-compression" />

## Buckets

**Export** a bucket to a backup archive you can download, and bring one back with **Import
bucket**. When you create a bucket on a local pool, Incendio warns you if the server has no
`core.storage_buckets_address`, without which nothing can reach the bucket.
