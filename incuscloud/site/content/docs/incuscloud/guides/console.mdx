The console at `https://cloud.example.com` is one view over every cluster, laid out as an inventory
in the style of vSphere. Each cluster's own UI, Incendio, runs inside it, so every Incus page is one
click away. What you see follows your permissions; the API and Incus decide every request.

## The side menu

- **Views**: Instances, Projects, Members, Networks, Storage. They change what the tree lists.
- **The inventory tree**: All clusters → each cluster → what the view lists: members and their
  instances, projects with their instances, networks or pools. Each row has its kind's icon,
  tinted by status, and running/total counts.
- **Platform pages**: Tenants, Kubernetes, Jobs, Replication, Backups, Balancing, and, for those who
  manage them, Backup targets, Users, Groups, Permissions, Service accounts, the Audit log and the
  Gateway log.
- **Your account**: your name opens your Profile.

Drag the side menu between 240 and 360 px wide; the view and width are remembered.

## Selection and tabs

The URL is the selection. Above the page is a breadcrumb back up the tree, then one row of tabs
for the object; what doesn't fit goes into **More**.

| Object | Tabs |
| --- | --- |
| All clusters | Summary (the dashboard), Clusters, Instances, Warnings |
| A cluster | Summary, Members, Groups, Projects, Networks, Storage, Operations, Access, Configure |
| A project | Instances, Profiles, Networks, Storage, Images, Usage, Access, Configure |

Tabs with several pages list them in a side menu. A cluster's **Configure** tab, for example, has
IncusCloud's settings for it, server and cluster settings, links, replicators, certificates,
logging, ACME and warnings.

## Access tabs

A cluster's or project's **Access** tab shows who has access and why: the platform roles, each
tenant's members, and the permission entries that reach it, each with the relation it gives in
Incus. Whoever holds `acl.manage` there can add and remove entries right on the tab.

## Incendio alone

Each cluster's Incendio is also served by itself at `https://cloud.example.com/clusters/<cluster>/ui/`,
signed in with your IncusCloud session.
